# Known-bad log signatures. Any match in the scanned window fails the gate.
# Not listed on purpose: SRMSG18224 ("consumer revoked re-balance listener") is logged at INFO on
# every normal rebalance, e.g. each KEDA scale-down. A consumer that never rejoins is caught by
# the "consumer group has live members" check in log-scan.sh instead.
# Format: <extended regex> ## <what it means / where it is documented in CLAUDE.md>
SRMSG00034 ## Kafka emitter demand exhausted — leaked credits after an Apicurio outage (Chaos finding 2)
SRMSG18249 ## Unhandled deserialization failure — Kafka consumer fail-stops permanently (Chaos finding 6)
SRMSG00019 ## Emitter not connected to its channel at startup (Issue #17, CPU-starved startup)
HHH000342 ## Hibernate could not obtain JDBC metadata at startup (Issue #17, CPU-starved startup)
too many clients already ## PostgreSQL max_connections exhausted (connection budget outage 2026-09-29)
acquisition timeout ## Agroal pool exhausted or holding dead connections (JDBC self-healing note)
could not be resolved ## nginx cannot resolve an upstream — wrong DNS resolver (dashboard 502, 2026-10-06)
Thread blocked ## Vert.x event loop blocked — missing @Blocking on a JAX-RS method
java\.lang\.OutOfMemoryError ## JVM heap exhausted (the bare word also appears in every JVM's -XX:+ExitOnOutOfMemoryError flag)
relation "[a-z_]+" does not exist ## Schema missing or reset while services were running (bootstrap-phase2.sh re-run)
Enlisted connection used without active transaction ## @Blocking + @Transactional on an @Incoming handler
kafka-consumer-channel.*DOWN ## Consumer retry budget exhausted — pod will be restarted by liveness
SRMSG18228 ## A Kafka channel reported a failure — with the default fail strategy the consumer stops for good while the pod stays Ready
SRMSG18231 ## A record waited longer than throttled.unprocessed-record-max-age.ms (60s) for its ack — the consumer is torn down (seen 2026-10-06: ledger-service retrying its DB write through an interconnect outage)
Kafka consumer polling thread unresponsive ## KafkaConsumerPollMonitor: the consumer was closed by a fatal channel failure (or its polling thread hung) for longer than kafka.consumer.liveness.stale-after — liveness goes DOWN and the pod is restarted (issue #24)
sent to DLQ ## transaction-processor rejected a transaction — since issue #25 only insufficient funds or an unknown account can cause this, and the generated load produces neither
